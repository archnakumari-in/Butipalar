PRIYANKA BEAUTY PARLOUR - WEBSITE
=================================
Kaise chalayein: index.html par double-click karein (browser me khulega).
Map aur fonts ke liye internet chahiye.

ZAROORI BADLAV
1) Phone number: js/script.js ke top par PHONE_INTL aur PHONE_SHOW badlein.
   Isi se Call button, WhatsApp button aur booking form sab chalenge.
2) Photos: images/ folder me apni asli photos daalein (jpg/png) aur
   index.html me src="images/hair.svg" ko src="images/hair.jpg" kar dein.
   Abhi illustrated placeholder images lage hain.
3) Rates: index.html me "Price list" section me rates apne hisab se badlein.
4) Reviews: "What our clients say" me sample reviews hain, asli reviews daalein.
5) Exact map pin: index.html me iframe ka src badal kar Google Maps ->
   Share -> Embed a map ka link paste karein (parlour ka exact pin).
6) Colours: css/style.css ke top me :root me.

Online daalne ke liye poora folder kisi bhi hosting (Netlify, GitHub Pages) par upload karein.
